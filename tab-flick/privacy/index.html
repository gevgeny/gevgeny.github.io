Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

TabFlick is designed to keep your browsing information on your Mac. The app and its browser extensions do not send your tabs, browsing history, or previews to us.

Who we are

TabFlick is developed and operated by Eugene Gluhotorenko. Questions about this policy or your privacy can be sent to e.gluhotorenko@gmail.com.

How TabFlick handles browser data

To show and manage your recent tabs, TabFlick handles tab titles, URLs, favicons, browser windows and tab state, thumbnail previews, and supported live-tab signals such as media playback, meeting microphone or camera state, and unread counts.

The browser extensions use their permissions to provide TabFlick’s features, including listing tabs, creating previews, detecting supported tab activity, and performing actions you request. This information stays on your device.

Chrome asks for access to all websites because capturing a preview of the tab you are looking at requires that permission. TabFlick does not read page content under it. A small script runs only on a fixed list of media and messaging sites (YouTube, Spotify, Google Meet, Zoom, Slack, Teams and a few others) to read playback state, meeting microphone or camera state, and the unread count shown in the page title.

The extensions communicate only with the TabFlick Mac app on the same computer through a local WebSocket connection at 127.0.0.1, using ports 43117–43121. The app’s server listens only on the loopback interface, so it is not accessible from the local network or the internet. For Chrome, the app verifies that the connection carries TabFlick’s Chrome Web Store extension origin. Every client must also complete a matching handshake before the app accepts the connection. We do not operate a server that receives your browser data, and we do not sell or share it.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TabFlick does not access or operate in private or incognito browser windows or tabs.

Website analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect aggregate traffic and performance statistics. It uses no cookies, local storage, or individual visitor profiles. We use these measurements to understand whether the site is useful and functioning properly.

Cloudflare processes this information on our behalf. You can read more in Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you contact us, we use the information you provide only to respond to your message and provide support. We keep it only as long as reasonably necessary.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information, or to object to certain processing. TabFlick does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Because browsing information handled by TabFlick stays on your device, we cannot access or delete it remotely. You can remove it by clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling the app and extensions. For information you sent directly to us, contact e.gluhotorenko@gmail.com.
