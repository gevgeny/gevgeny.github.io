Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

TabFlick is designed to keep your browsing information on your Mac. The app and its browser extensions do not send your tabs, browsing history, or previews off your Mac.

Who develops TabFlick

TabFlick is developed and operated by Eugene Gluhotorenko. Questions about this policy or your privacy can be sent to e.gluhotorenko@gmail.com.

How TabFlick handles browser data

To show and manage your recent tabs, TabFlick handles tab titles, URLs, favicons, browser windows and tab state, thumbnail previews, and whether a tab is playing sound or muted, as reported by the browser.

The browser extensions use their permissions to provide TabFlick’s features, including listing tabs, creating previews, and performing actions you request, such as switching, closing, pinning or muting a tab. This information stays on your device.

Chrome asks for access to all websites because capturing a preview of the tab you are looking at requires that permission. TabFlick does not read page content under it, and runs no scripts inside the pages you visit.

To show unread messages, TabFlick reads only the browser tab’s title, such as “(3) WhatsApp”, where the site itself puts its unread count. It does this only for these sites: Gmail, WhatsApp Web, Messenger, LinkedIn, Microsoft Teams, Proton Mail, Instagram and Discord. It never reads the page or your messages, and the title stays on your Mac like all other tab information. You can turn this off in the app’s Settings under “Tab activity”.

The extensions communicate only with the TabFlick Mac app on the same computer through a local WebSocket connection at 127.0.0.1, using ports 43117–43121. The app’s server listens only on the loopback interface, so it is not accessible from the local network or the internet. For Chrome, the app verifies that the connection carries TabFlick’s Chrome Web Store extension origin. Every client must also complete a matching handshake before the app accepts the connection. TabFlick operates no server that receives your browser data, and never sells or shares it.

To show a tab’s icon, the app may load it from that tab’s website, as your browser does.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TabFlick does not access or operate in private or incognito browser windows or tabs.

Website analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect aggregate traffic and performance statistics. It uses no cookies, local storage, or individual visitor profiles. These measurements are used only to understand whether the site is useful and functioning properly.

Cloudflare processes this information on TabFlick’s behalf. You can read more in Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Contact

Information you provide when contacting TabFlick is used only to answer your message and provide support, and is kept only as long as reasonably necessary.

Your data

Because browsing information handled by TabFlick stays on your device, it cannot be accessed or deleted remotely. You can remove it yourself by clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling the app and extensions.
