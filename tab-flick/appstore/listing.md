### Notes

```
TabFlick is a tab switcher for web browsers. It reaches the browser through a companion extension; for review, Safari needs nothing else installed because its extension ships inside the app.

To test with Safari:
1. Launch TabFlick. The welcome screen opens — click Get Started.
2. Next to Safari, click Connect, then turn on the TabFlick extension in Safari Settings → Extensions and allow it on all websites.
3. Open a few tabs in Safari, then hold Control and press Tab: the switcher shows the recent tabs; release Control to switch.
4. Option-Tab opens the switcher from any app, across all connected browsers.

Trial and purchase: the 14-day trial starts automatically on first launch — there is no trial purchase. Its length and the price are disclosed on the welcome screen before anything else. The only in-app purchase is the one-time "TabFlick Full Unlock" (non-consumable), available from the welcome screen, the menu bar (Buy) and the Activate window; Restore Purchase is next to it.

The extensions for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera (Chrome Web Store) and Firefox (Firefox Add-ons) work the same way; the app links to them from its Connect screen.
```
