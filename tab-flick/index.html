Built for everyday browsing

Native on macOS

TabFlick runs as a native Mac app, so its switcher can appear over any app and take you directly to the browser window and tab you need.

Manage tabs without opening them

Hover any preview to close that tab, or unpin a pinned one, without opening it or losing your place.

Make it yours

Configure your shortcuts, how many tabs to show, the accent colour, and whether tabs switch live as you cycle.

Most-recent tabs first

TabFlick keeps the tabs you just used closest, so the one you want is usually only one tap away.
